## What the article covers

- How keywords in the title, description, tags, category and attributes work together to match a listing to a search.
- Why the category and each attribute behave like extra tags, so tags should not repeat them.
- Title advice: short, clear, most descriptive words first, written for a buyer scanning results. Includes before/after title examples.
- Description advice: work the main keywords into the opening sentences without pasting the title.
- A list of tagging dos and don'ts, covering the 13-tag and 20-character limits, multi-word phrases, long-tail terms, regional phrases, repeats, misspellings, plurals and languages.
- Seven angles for brainstorming tags: descriptive, materials and techniques, who it's for, shopping occasions, solution-oriented, style, size.
