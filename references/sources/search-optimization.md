# Etsy Search Optimization: Listing Anatomy and Official Checklist

Two Etsy Seller Handbook articles, both dated August 26, 2025 and fetched 2026-06-12:

- **The Anatomy of a Well-Crafted Etsy Listing**: https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1347574487014
- **Checklist: Optimize Your Shop for Etsy Search**: https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/366470356778

This file is a source note. The articles themselves are Etsy's content and are not copied here; follow the links to read them.

## What the articles cover

- How search ranking weighs the whole listing, plus shop-level signals such as customer service history, shop policies and US shipping price.
- Title tips: name the item, lead with its key traits, stay under about 15 words, and leave out subjective words, repeats and price or sale information.
- A rule of thumb for tags: use natural phrases a person would actually type into a search engine, and use all 13.
- Titles and tags belong in the shop's own language, because Etsy translates them for shoppers.
- First-photo requirements for search: minimum and recommended pixel sizes, a clean background, no text overlays or collages.
- Description structure: essential details first, short paragraphs or bullets, the story at the end.
- Ongoing upkeep: refresh titles and tags on slow listings and before seasons and holidays, and use the search visibility page in Shop Manager.
