## What the article covers

- What attributes are: structured options that depend on the listing's category, unlike free-text tags.
- How they affect search: they help match queries and they power the sidebar filters, so a listing without an attribute does not show up when a buyer filters by it.
- Which attributes most categories share (color, holiday, occasion) and examples of category-specific ones.
- How to fill them in: add every attribute that applies, skip those that don't, describe what the product is and not how it might be used, and pick the closest option when none is exact.
